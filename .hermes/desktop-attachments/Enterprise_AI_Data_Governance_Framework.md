# Enterprise AI & Data Governance Framework for Agentic AI Projects

## Purpose

A reusable governance framework for **evaluating, designing,
implementing, and auditing AI agentic systems** by combining the
concepts shown in the provided governance maps with recognized
standards.

## Governance Stack

### 1. Principles

-   OECD AI Principles
-   UNESCO AI Ethics
-   Fairness
-   Transparency
-   Human oversight
-   Accountability
-   Safety

### 2. Regulatory Layer

-   EU AI Act classification
-   GDPR/privacy
-   Organization-specific legal obligations

### 3. Management System

Primary references: - ISO/IEC 42001 - ISO/IEC 38507 - NIST AI RMF -
ISO/IEC 42005 - ISO/IEC 23894

Outputs: - AI Governance Charter - AI Policy - AI Risk Register - AI
Inventory

### 4. Data Governance

Based on DAMA-DMBOK and ISO 27001.

Evaluate: - Data ownership - Data quality - Lineage - Metadata - Access
control - Security - Retention - Data contracts

### 5. Agent Governance

For every agent document: - Business objective - Owner - Scope - Allowed
actions - Restricted actions - Required approvals - Human-in-the-loop
checkpoints - Tools - Memory strategy - Knowledge sources - Failure
handling - Escalation policy

### 6. AI Lifecycle

1.  Business case
2.  Risk assessment
3.  Data readiness
4.  Model selection
5.  Agent design
6.  Tool integration
7.  Testing
8.  Security review
9.  Deployment
10. Monitoring
11. Continuous improvement
