# Strategy: AI Governance for Agentic Workflows

## Vision
To enable safe, autonomous agent deployment in production while maintaining human auditability and risk control.

## Strategic Priorities (Next 1-2 Quarters)
1. **Security:** Establish safeguards against prompt injection, data leakage, and unauthorized tool execution.
2. **Reliability & Cost Optimization:** Define thresholds for token usage, loop detection, and accuracy benchmarks to prevent "runaway" agents.
3. **Compliance & Governance:** Create a transparent audit trail of agent decisions and actions for regulatory and internal review.

## Scope
- **Primary Users:** Internal Engineering Teams and Product Managers.
- **Maturity:** Proactive framework to be implemented and refined via pilot projects prior to formal stakeholder review.

## Non-Goals
- Defining governance for non-agentic LLM chatbots (simple RAG/Q&A).
- Creating a legally binding external regulatory standard (this is an internal framework).
